🌡️ O2 Sensor Ground Types (P0134 / P0154 Fix)

Case-Grounded (Flat Black 4-Pin Plug): Grounds TAN wire through exhaust threads.

Isolated-Ground on 99–02 Truck P01 (Square White Plug): Sensor body is isolated! TAN low-ref splice must connect to 99–02 Truck C1 Blue Pin 63 (or engine ground) or O2s read dead.

03–07 P59 & Gen IV/V (E38/E40/E67/E92): Each O2 sensor has its own dedicated ECM low-ref pin (and 03–07 P59 switches O2 heaters low-side on C2 Green Pins 72 & 74 — never ground P59 O2 heater wires to the block!).

🚨 Starts for 2 Seconds & Immediately Dies
Cause & Fix: 99% of the time VATS (Vehicle Anti-Theft) is still enabled in the tune (cuts injector pulse after 2 sec). Also try unplugging the MAF sensor — if it idles unplugged, your MAF calibration doesn't match the sensor or there is a big post-MAF intake leak.
🚨 Cranks Strong, Zero Spark & Zero Pop
Cause & Fix: Your Pink Ignition 12V+ wire is dropping to 0V while the key is held in START (CRANK)! Measure voltage on PCM C1-19 / X1-19 / X1-51 and coil Pink wires while cranking — move your relay trigger to a true Run+Crank ignition terminal.
🚨 Tachometer Dead or Reads Half RPM
Cause & Fix: On Gen III/IV, solder a 1kΩ (1,000 Ohm) pull-up resistor between the Tach output wire (C2-10 / X1-48 / C1-48 / C1-25) and 12V+ Key-On, and set your tach to 4-Cyl (2 PPR) mode! (Note: Gen V E92 X2-40 outputs a raw 58X crank signal and requires a tach adapter like Dakota Digital SGI-100BT unless reconfigured in OS).
🚨 4L60E / 4L80E Stuck in 3rd Gear or No Lockup
Cause & Fix: Feed Pin E (Pink wire) on the 20-pin round trans plug with 15A Switched 12V+! For Torque Converter Lockup on Gen III, C1 Blue Pin 33 (Purple) needs 12V+ when OFF the brakes and 0V when pressing the brakes.
