⚡ Standalone Fuse Block, Relays & OBD2 Port Wiring

Clean schematics and pinouts for powering Gen III (97–07), Gen IV (05–15 E38/E40/E67), and Gen V (14+ E92) standalone swaps.

🔋 1. Gen III Standalone 4-Fuse & 2-Relay Box (LT1Swap Standard)

Works for 99–07 Vortec 4.8/5.3/6.0, 97–02 LS1, 04 GTO, and 94–97 LT1. One Main Ignition Relay feeds 3 switched fuses, plus 1 Constant B+ fuse and 1 Fuel Pump Relay.

[BATTERY 12V+ CONSTANT] ──┬──► [FUSE 1: 10A/15A CONSTANT B+] ──► PCM C1-20 & C1-57 (ORN) + OBD2 Pin 16 │ ├──► [IGNITION RELAY Pin 30] │ ├─ Pin 86: Vehicle Key Switch 12V+ (HOT IN RUN & CRANK!) │ ├─ Pin 85: Chassis Ground │ └─ Pin 87 (Switched 12V+ Output to 3 Bussed Fuses): │ ├──► [FUSE 2: 15A INJ/COIL BANK 1] ──► Pink: Coils 1,3,5,7 & Inj 1,3,5,7 │ ├──► [FUSE 3: 15A INJ/COIL BANK 2] ──► Pink: Coils 2,4,6,8 & Inj 2,4,6,8 │ └──► [FUSE 4: 15A PCM/MAF/O2/TRANS] ─► PCM C1-19 & C1-75 (PNK), MAF Pin D, │ Front O2 Heaters (Pin D), Trans Pin E │ └──► [FUEL PUMP RELAY Pin 30 (via 20A Fuse)] ├─ Pin 85: PCM Fuel Pump Trigger (C2 Pin 9 DK GRN/WHT = 12V+ Output!) ├─ Pin 86: Chassis Ground └─ Pin 87: Gray 14-AWG Wire to Electric Fuel Pump (+)
⚠️ #1 First-Start Mistake: Your key switch wire feeding Relay Pin 86 MUST stay 12V+ hot during BOTH RUN and CRANK (START)! Always verify with a meter while cranking.

🛠️ First-Start No-Start & Swap Troubleshooter

Fast, proven fixes for the 6 most common LS & LT standalone wiring issues.

🚨 1. Fires for 2 Seconds and Immediately Dies

99% Cause: VATS anti-theft is still enabled in the tune, or fuel pump loses power after prime.
  • Confirm VATS is disabled in HP Tuners / EFILive (VATS cuts injector pulse ~2s after start).
  • Unplug MAF test: If it idles with MAF unplugged, your MAF calibration is mismatched or there is a post-MAF intake leak.
  • Verify steady 58 PSI rail pressure after the 2-second prime.

🚨 2. Cranks Strong, Zero Spark & Zero Pop

Cause: Switched 12V+ Pink wire drops to 0V while cranking!
  • Meter PCM Pink (C1-19 / X1-19 / X1-51) and Coil Pin D while cranking. Must stay >10V.
  • Move Ignition Relay trigger off an Accessory/Run-only lug to a true IGN 1 (Hot in RUN & START) lug.
  • Verify all cylinder head / block ground lugs (especially rear of driver head) are bolted to bare metal.

🚨 3. Tachometer Dead or Reads Half RPM

  • Dead Tach Fix: Solder a 1kΩ 1/4W pull-up resistor between the White tach wire (C2-10 / X1-48) and Key-On 12V+.
  • Half RPM Fix: Factory GM LS tach output is a 4-cylinder pulse (2 pulses/rev). Set gauge to 4-Cyl mode or change pulses in tune.

🚨 4. 4L60E / 4L80E Stuck in 3rd Gear or No Lockup

  • Stuck in 3rd Gear: Feed Pink Pin E on the 20-pin round trans plug with a 15A Key-On 12V+ fuse!
  • No Converter Lockup: Gen III C1 Blue Pin 33 (Purple) MUST have 12V+ when OFF the brakes and 0V when pressing the brakes.

🚨 5. DBW Throttle Dead / Reduced Engine Power

  • 03–07 P59: PCM OS year, TAC Module, and Pedal MUST be a matched set (2003 metal-tab TAC will NOT talk to 06–07 OS).
  • E38 Gen IV: Truck and Camaro pedals swap Pins D and F (check Tab 4 Pedal Matrix).

🚨 6. Alternator Not Charging

  • 99–02 4-Pin Alternator: Wire Pin B (L-terminal) to PCM C2 Red Pin 15, OR through a 470Ω 1/2W resistor to Key-On 12V+.
  • 03–07 2-Pin Alternator: Controlled by PCM (C1-15 & C2-75). Never connect straight 12V+ without a resistor!
